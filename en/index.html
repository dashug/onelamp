FAQ

Apps are not being blocked?

Open Onelamp and finish the Screen Time authorization in Settings. If you ever turned off Onelamp's access under iPhone Settings › Screen Time, authorize it again. The permission is only used to block what you choose.

Pro is missing after a new phone or reinstall?

In Onelamp, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iPhone Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, choose Onelamp and cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

Where is my data stored?

Your rules, focus history and settings are stored only on this iPhone. We run no servers and cannot see them. Export or erase them in the app under Settings › Data. See the Privacy Policy.
