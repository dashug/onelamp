Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Developer: 【待填 DEVELOPER_NAME】

This policy explains how the app handles your data. "We" means the developer named above.

Summary

  • Your data stays on your device. The app has no accounts and we run no servers. Your rules, focus history and settings are stored only on this iPhone and are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, and it does not track you.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see who you are or how you pay.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time. The controls are in the app's Settings and History.

Data stored on your device

The following data is created as you use the app. It is stored only on this device and shared between the app, its widgets and its blocking extensions. We cannot access any of it.

  • Focus rules: the rule name, and the system tokens that stand for the apps, categories and websites you picked. Used for blocking those items during a focus session or a scheduled lock.
  • Focus history: start and end time, planned length, time actually focused, Pomodoro rounds, whether the session was completed or ended early, how many times a block was shown, the note you typed, the name of the rule used. Used for history, statistics, the daily goal, streaks and achievements.
  • Quick-start templates and scheduled locks: name, length or time window, repeat days, the linked rule; a scheduled lock also keeps its own copy of the tokens picked in that rule. Used for starting a session in one tap, and locking automatically on schedule.
  • Settings: exit-challenge method, daily goal, reminder time, your custom block-screen text, unlocked achievements. Used for running the app the way you set it up.
  • Pro status: the purchase result Apple reports to the app, see "Purchases and subscriptions" below. Used for unlocking Pro features on this device.

This data lives in the local storage iOS provides to the app and is covered by the system's Data Protection.

Screen Time access

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time API and needs your authorization as an individual. It is used only to block the apps, categories and websites you picked yourself, during a focus session or a scheduled lock you set up.

  • Apple hands your picks to the app only as opaque tokens. The app can use a token to block something, but it cannot read which app or website the token stands for.
  • The app does not read your app usage time, your browsing history or anything inside other apps.
  • The "blocked" count is just a number: during a session it goes up by one when you tap the button on the block screen, and it does not record which app that was.
  • You can turn off Screen Time access for this app in the system Settings at any time, or simply delete the app. Blocking stops working once you do.

Notifications and other system features

  • Notifications: when a focus session ends, when a Pomodoro switches between work and break, and the daily reminder if you turn it on. These are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not pass through any server and are never used for marketing.
  • Live Activities, the Dynamic Island and widgets: show the time left, the rule name and today's progress, rendered on your device by the system.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: can start or end a focus session. When you use Siri by voice, Apple processes the voice request under its own privacy policy.
  • Photos: the system asks for permission to add to your library only when you choose Save Image for a focus card in the share sheet. The app never reads your photo library.

Purchases and subscriptions

The app may offer optional paid features called Pro. They are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase, and Apple handles payment, renewal and refunds. The app only learns from the system whether this Apple Account owns Pro, which plan it is, when it expires and whether it will renew, and uses that to unlock features on this device. None of it is sent to us.

We never receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. All we can see is the sales statistics Apple gives developers, which do not identify you.

Export and deletion

  • Export: under Data in Settings you can export your focus history as a CSV or JSON file and save it wherever you choose through the system share sheet.
  • Delete records: swipe left on any entry in History to delete it. On the free tier History lists only the last 7 days; older records can be deleted together with "Delete Older Records" at the end of the list.
  • Clear focus history: deletes all focus records at once and keeps your rules and settings.
  • Erase all data: deletes rules, templates, scheduled locks, focus history, achievements and settings, and lifts any scheduled lock that is active. While a focus session is running you need to end it first. Your purchase belongs to your Apple Account and is not affected.
  • Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored on this device.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS includes this app's data in your device backup. Backups are managed by you and Apple, and we cannot access them.

The app offers no cloud sync, and your data never appears on any server of ours.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up, and no name, phone number, email address or other identity data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and no advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. We do not hold your data, so nothing is transferred across borders and we could not hand it over at anyone's request either.
  • If you chose to share analytics with app developers under Privacy & Security in the system Settings, Apple gives developers anonymous crash and performance statistics. Apple handles this, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. When you start a feedback email from inside the app, the draft is prefilled with the app version, the iOS version and similar troubleshooting details, which you can read or delete before sending. You can ask us to delete the correspondence.

Children

The app is a tool for managing your own device. It is not a parental-control product and has no way to view or monitor anyone else. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a minor, please use the app with your parent's or guardian's permission and guidance.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that makes data leave your device, such as sync, analytics or a third-party service, we will first update this policy and the privacy details on the App Store and tell you inside the app, and only then turn the feature on. Small wording changes only update the date above.

You can read this policy at any time from the app's Settings.
